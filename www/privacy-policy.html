Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Overview

Tic Tac Toe — A Premium Indie Showdown ("the App") is a free game built with love for the Rohingya community. It offers three ways to play: 2 Player (same device), Vs Computer (fully offline), and Play Online (real-time multiplayer against a friend). This policy explains what data is involved in each mode.

Offline Modes (2 Player & Vs Computer)

These modes run entirely on your device. No account or sign-in is required, and no personal data, location data, analytics, or advertising SDKs are used. Player names you type and your scores are stored only in your browser's localStorage and never leave your device.

Online Multiplayer

When you use "Play Online," the App sends the display name you enter and your game moves to our server so it can relay them to the friend you're playing against. This is the minimum data needed to run a live match. Specifically:

  • Room state (board, whose turn it is, and scores for that room) is held in server memory and is not linked to any account.
  • The display name you enter is shared only with the other player in your room.
  • Rooms and their data are automatically deleted after a few hours of inactivity.
  • No email, contact info, or precise location is collected.
  • No advertising or third-party tracking SDKs are used.

Internet Connection

2 Player and Vs Computer work fully offline after first load. Play Online requires an internet connection to reach our server. The App also loads the Archivo typeface from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) on first load.

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction) beyond the temporary, non-identifying display name used during an online match.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this policy, the new version will be posted here with a revised date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
